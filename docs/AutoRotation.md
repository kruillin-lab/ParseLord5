# Auto Rotation

Auto Rotation is a WrathCombo system that can automatically perform your rotation with active input from you.
In ParseLord5, Auto Rotation behavior currently comes from the WrathCombo base unless ParseLord5 explicitly changes it later.
Its settings are in the left column of the WrathCombo configuration window.

## Setting Up Auto Rotation

> [!TIP]
> At the very bottom of the settings page is Throttle Delay. This determines how fast
> Auto Rotation "presses the button." Setting this too low can reduce your FPS.

### Enable Auto Rotation

- Enable Auto Rotation
- Use sub-options to control behavior such as only activating in combat.
- Set your delay for how quickly Auto Rotation activates at the beginning of combat.

### Damage Settings

- Choose your targeting mode.
- Set the number of targets required to perform AoE.
- Use the sub-options to control how targeting works.

### Healing Settings

- Choose your targeting mode.
- Set your healing thresholds. Auto Rotation will not use heals until HP is below these thresholds.
- Choose how many targets are required for AoE.
- Set up other settings for Resurrection, Esuna, Smart Kardia Management for Sage, and out-of-combat regens on tanks.
